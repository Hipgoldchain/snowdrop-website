# Snowdrop Website

## Copy voice

These rules bind every copy change in this repo. They are taken verbatim from positioning v6 sections 6.1 to 6.6.

### 6.1 The rhythm rule (read this one first)

**Vary sentence length, hard.** The deepest tell of machine-written copy is not any single word, it is that every sentence runs to roughly the same length, so the rhythm goes flat and the reader stops trusting it without knowing why. Real writing is ragged. A four word sentence lands after a long one.

This rule catches more than any banned-words list. "No prep needed, bring coffee" works precisely because it is short and sits next to something longer.

### 6.2 Mechanical bans

- **No em dashes. Ever.** Em dashes (—) are banned outright, no exceptions. No hyphens used as sentence breaks either. Use commas.
- No en dashes used as punctuation (en dashes are only acceptable inside number ranges like £140k–£337k).
- No colons in headlines.
- No exclamation marks.
- No bolding mid-paragraph for emphasis.

### 6.3 Banned constructions

- **"X, not Y" contrasts use a comma, never a full stop.** Write "Built for small businesses, not the FTSE 100." The full-stop version ("Built for small businesses. Not the FTSE 100.") reads as machine-written.
- No sentence fragments used as emphasis.
- No "It's not just X, it's Y."
- No three-part lists where the third item is the twist.
- No "Whether you're a builder or an accountant."
- No rhetorical question as an opener.
- No "Here's the thing", "The truth is", "Let's be honest".
- No "That's where we come in."
- No symmetrical clause pairs. "Built for speed, designed for scale" is the sound of nobody.
- No alliterative triplets.

### 6.4 Banned vocabulary

Carried from v5: diagnostic, wedge, leverage, workflow-first, enterprise-grade, operational efficiency, engagement, stakeholder, proposition, multi-brain analysis, SME/SMEs.

Added in v6: unlock, elevate, empower, transform, seamless, effortless, simply, robust, harness, navigate, landscape, journey, delve, streamline, supercharge, game-changer, cutting-edge, "in today's fast-paced world".

### 6.5 Protected lines, do not improve

- "No prep needed, bring coffee."
- "No tool you'll never log into again."
- "We'll only sell you the roadmap if it's a fit."
- "Built for small businesses, not the FTSE 100."
- The tagline: "Growth systems for small businesses that work in weeks, not months."

### 6.6 Audience language

"Small businesses" survives in the tagline and the About H2. Elsewhere prefer "businesses" or "growing businesses". "SME" never appears in customer-facing copy, ads included.
